# Lavaloon Terminal — System Feature Documentation

See the complete institutional specification in [`docs/SYSTEM_DOCUMENTATION.md`](docs/SYSTEM_DOCUMENTATION.md).

### Summary of Modules
1. **Overview Dashboard (`F1`)**: Macro dataset strip, 4 KPI cards, verified regulatory filings table.
2. **Entity Explorer (`F2`)**: 3-region Palantir company dossier, audited highlights grid, business segments, Item 1A risks.
3. **Financial Intelligence (`F3`)**: Comparative multi-statement matrix, currency isolation (USD Billions vs INR Crores).
4. **Knowledge Graph (`F4`)**: Cytoscape ontology with Dijkstra pathfinder ($1/\text{Lift}$).
5. **GraphRAG Copilot (`F5`)**: Multi-hop BFS reasoning with grounded triples and statistical profiles.
6. **Due Diligence Dossier (`F6`)**: 10-K grounded diligence memo with Markdown/PDF export.
7. **Options Desk (`F7`)**: Black-Scholes-Merton pricing engine & analytical Greeks matrix.
8. **System Diagnostics (`F8`)**: Real-time architecture telemetry and engine status.
